## Three findings are defects in the session that ran the audit

This audit was run mid-session, between the last content pull request and the
changelog cut. Three of its findings are about work merged hours earlier, which
is the argument for running it before the tag rather than after.

**The README's root-model prose was false for half the roots.** `v2.80.0` adds
a "What a project picks" section (#1471, PR #1522). It stated that "an extra is
one file plus the core tier". Measured: 10 of the 20 opt-in roots exceed
core+1 — `backend-webhooks` is core+5, `platform-github`, `backend-edge` and
`backend-microservices` core+4, `base-two-pass-review` and `platform-gitlab`
core+3.

Nothing caught it because the counts sat outside the generated blocks. The
reviewer's control: rewriting "20 orthogonal templates" to "99" left
`sync.py --check` reporting **All files in sync** and smoke at 29/29.

Fixed during the audit rather than filed. The sentence is now generated from
the resolver, and the same control fails: the falsified count exits 1. A second
control confirms the span is measured and not restated — a synthetic seven-file
extra moves the generated text from "1 to 5" to "1 to 7".

**The chain-ceiling raise reversed most of the only reduction ever made.** A
full diff replay of `tests/chain-budget.txt` across its 21 commits: **425 raises
against 37 lowers**. All 37 lowers are one commit, `f93dbe8` (#1508, 2026-09-05,
−33,869 bytes). `916c5e3` (#1521, 2026-09-06) raised all 37 roots by 29,193
bytes — 86% of that reduction, inside 24 hours. Net all-time **+226,601 bytes**.

The raise was made honestly and in the same change as the growth, which is what
the ratchet asks. The finding is that the ratchet only prices growth: #1462 is
open, and the reviewer's control confirmed it — raising `stack-django`'s ceiling
by 100,000 leaves `SYS-12` passing with byte-identical notes.

**Today's enumeration fix landed on one check while its siblings kept walking
the disk.** #1515 fixed `SYS-07` and #1520 fixed the shipped comment-layout
check by asking git what belongs to the repository. `all_template_files()` and
`run_conformance.py:74` were not touched. Filed as #1525, and it is the audit's
most serious finding.

## Current bottleneck

`all_template_files()` (#1525). It defines the corpus for six authoring checks
from a hardcoded 12-directory non-recursive listing. The control planted a
template declaring the duplicate ID `base-git` and an empty section, registered
it in the manifest, and shipped it past smoke, sync, resolve, redundancy and
conformance — all green — while the printed corpus counts stayed at 74 files
and 366 IDs throughout.

That is the failure the counts were added to prevent. A stale count and a
correct one are indistinguishable when the enumerator never looked, and the
`Inspected` floor guards zero rather than collapse: 59 of 63 `count()` calls
use the default floor of 1.

## Method note

Seven isolated subagents, each with a clean context, its own role prompt, and
its own dimension's prior findings. Every reviewer was instructed to verify
each finding adversarially, name the control it ran, and drop what it could not
substantiate; the Testing and Architecture reviewers were additionally required
to plant a violation, confirm the mutation landed, run the check, and revert.

All mutation controls were reverted. `git status` after fan-out showed only the
changelog cut in progress; the planted `templates/rogue.md` and its manifest
entry were confirmed gone before anything was committed.

The three findings against this session's own work were verified by hand
against the tree before being accepted, not taken from the reviewers on trust.
