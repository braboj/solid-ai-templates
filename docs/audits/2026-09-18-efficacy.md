Does a generated context file improve the result? The question, the verdict rule and the
metric directions were fixed before any trial ran, in
`docs/design/efficacy-benchmark.md`.

**Every interval below is descriptive.** At K = 3 no arrangement of paired differences
reaches conventional significance, which the design states and this report repeats
rather than footnotes.

install left the app unable to boot. It built more than the task asked for: more files,
and took more tokens, time and cost to do it. The judge saw none of it as better design,
readability or maintainability; every win is something a tool counts.

**Templates' short file, Worse.** It built more than the task asked for: more files. The
judge saw none of it as better design, readability or maintainability; every win is
something a tool counts.

**Templates' hybrid file, No.** It built more than the task asked for: more files and
more code, and took more turns, tokens, time and cost to do it. The judge saw none of it
as better design, readability or maintainability.

**Hand-written file, No.** The judge saw none of it as better design, readability or
maintainability.

Reused from an earlier round: full (3 trials) and hand (3 trials). Pairing them with
this round's trials, across days: full − none, hand − none, short − hand and hybrid −
full. The model is pinned by exact id; the day is the residual confound.

Together: neither file added quality. K = 3, one project: a signal, not proof.

Between the files, 1 to 10: full − hand 3.4 (4 won, 11 failed); short − hand 3.6 (4 won,
10 failed); hybrid − full 7.4 (5 won, 2 failed). A win or a fail is a metric whose
interval separated the pair in its declared direction, each counting once; the score is
1 + 9 × wins ÷ (wins + fails), digests the verdict vector at the end, and decides
nothing.

Caveats: 15 trials, 15 judged; reused from an earlier round: full and hand; escalation
to K = 5 not owed, so K stays 3; withdrawn: nothing; lost and re-run: none-1, completed;
none-3, completed; reached past their workspace: hand-3 and hybrid-3; the judge is
checked only by its evidence lines.
