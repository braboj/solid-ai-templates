## Method note

Seven isolated reviewers, each with its own context window, its own dimension's
prior findings, and an instruction to verify every finding with a control that
must fail if the finding is false. Two reviewers re-took their figures from a
worktree pinned at `6e2e572` after a parallel session dirtied the working tree
mid-run, which is the practice `CLAUDE.md` §6.2 asks for. One reviewer measured
`sync --check` exit 1 at 08:31, established it was a concurrent reviewer's
mutation rather than a defect, and reported it as not a finding.

Two reviewers reported that their own instruments returned false results before
the tree did — a locale-decoded pipe, a stem collision in a file walker, and a
grep whose phrase list matched nothing. Each was caught by a control rather
than by inspection.
